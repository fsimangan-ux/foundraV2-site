Common questions.

Is this really a one-time payment?
Yes. $19 once. We never charge your card again. We're tired of subscriptions for everything, so we built ours differently.
What's the difference between Free and Full Report?
The free preview shows you what licenses and permits exist for your business — including descriptions and whether each is required, recommended, or optional. The $19 Full Report adds the direct .gov links to apply, detailed cost estimates, a downloadable PDF you keep forever, and an online dashboard to track your progress.
Can I see the free preview before I pay?
Yes. You don't even need to create an account. Just search your business type and state on the platform page — you'll see your full list of requirements with descriptions. If you want the application links, costs, and PDF, you upgrade for $19.
What if my business changes or expands?
Each $19 report is for one business in one state. If you start a second business or expand to a new state, you'd purchase a new report. Most founders only need one.
Will the report stay accurate as regulations change?
Yes. We monitor regulatory changes (federal and state) and email all paid users quarterly with any updates affecting their report. Free, included.
Can I get a refund if it's not useful?
Yes. 30-day no-questions-asked refund. Email info@foundra.us and we'll send your money back. We'd rather have happy non-customers than unhappy customers.
Is this legal advice?
No. Foundra is informational. We tell you which licenses and permits exist for your business type and state, and link you to official government sources. For specific legal questions about your unique situation, see a business attorney.
Why is the price $19?
Honestly? Because it's less than a tank of gas, less than dinner for two, and way less than the $300+ you'd pay a lawyer for the same info — but high enough that we can keep the lights on and keep improving Foundra.
When does paid checkout actually start?
Q3 2026. During beta (right now), everything is free. We're using this time to make sure the product is rock solid before charging anyone.
