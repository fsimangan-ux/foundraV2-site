What's actually included in the free plan?

Everything you need to understand what's required to start a business: industry-specific checklists, cost ranges, verified federal links to .gov applications, and direct links to state agency portals. Free forever, no credit card.

Is Foundra a substitute for an attorney?

No. Foundra is an informational tool that helps you understand what's typically required. For advice specific to your situation, consult a licensed attorney or accountant. Our Business+ tier includes expert consultation hours with vetted attorneys.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Pro and Business+ are month-to-month with no contracts. Cancel anytime, no questions asked.

How accurate is the information?

Federal links (IRS, FMCSA, DEA, etc.) are verified .gov URLs. State portal links go to official agency landing pages. Industry-specific requirements are reviewed by licensed attorneys before publication. We update our database monthly. That said, regulations change — always verify with the relevant agency before acting.
