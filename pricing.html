Common questions.

When does Pro actually launch?
Pro and Business+ tiers launch in Q3 2026. Waitlist members get 50% off their first 3 months — that's $24.50/month for Pro and $49.50/month for Business+ during your first quarter.
Why $49/month for Pro? Other tools are cheaper.
Most "cheaper" tools are paperwork filers (LegalZoom, ZenBusiness) that charge per filing. Foundra is different — we tell you what you need AND keep you compliant year-round. One missed renewal can cost more than a year of Pro. A single attorney consultation costs $200-500. We think $49/month is honestly priced for what you get.
What's the difference between Pro and Business+?
Pro is for founders managing their own businesses. Business+ is for professionals (accountants, attorneys, consultants) managing compliance for multiple clients. If you're running 1-3 of your own businesses, Pro is right. If you're managing compliance for 10+ businesses or clients, Business+ pays for itself.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Monthly plans cancel anytime — you'll keep access until the end of your billing period. Annual plans get a prorated refund for unused months. No questions asked.
Do I really only see 3 licenses on the free tier?
Yes. The free tier shows the top 3 required licenses so you can see Foundra's quality. The complete checklist (typically 7-15 items per business type, plus things most founders miss like FinCEN BOI reporting) is part of Pro. We'd rather be honest about this than bait-and-switch you later.
Is Foundra legal advice?
No. Foundra is informational software. We help you understand which licenses, permits, and compliance steps apply to your business — but for legal questions specific to your situation, consult a licensed attorney in your state. Foundra is the layer that should exist before you need a lawyer, not a replacement for one.
What happens to my data?
Your data is yours. We don't sell it to third parties, ever. See our privacy policy for details.
Will the free tier always exist?
Yes. We're committed to a free tier that gives meaningful value forever. We believe every founder deserves to know what licenses they need — even if they can't afford Pro.
