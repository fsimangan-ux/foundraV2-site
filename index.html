How it works

More than a checklist. An ongoing system.

Other tools give you a checklist and forget you exist. Foundra is built for the full lifecycle — from "what do I need?" to "what's renewing this month?"

i.

Discover what you need

Describe your business in plain English. Foundra’s AI matches you to verified federal and state requirements — with direct links to .gov applications.

ii.

Track your progress

Pro members get a personal dashboard. Save your checklist, check items off as you complete them, and see your compliance status across multiple businesses or states.

iii.

Stay compliant year after year

License renewals, annual reports, regulation changes — Foundra Pro alerts you 90, 60, and 30 days before every deadline. So you never get blindsided by a fine you could have prevented.
