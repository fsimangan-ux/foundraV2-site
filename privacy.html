Privacy policy

Last updated: January 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Foundra, Inc. ("Foundra," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at foundra.us and our related services (the "Service").

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you contact us, sign up for our waitlist, or use our platform:

  • Contact information (name, email address) when you reach out or sign up
  • Business information (business type, state) when you use our compliance navigator
  • Search queries you enter into the platform
  • Technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and device information

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Service
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Send you updates about Foundra (you can unsubscribe at any time)
  • Analyze usage patterns to improve the platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Information sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate Foundra (e.g., email delivery, analytics)
  • Government agencies if legally required
  • Successors in the event of a merger or acquisition

4. Cookies and tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and analyze how the Service is used. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not work properly.

5. Data security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, email hello@foundra.us.

7. Children's privacy

Foundra is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We'll post the updated version with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email us at hello@foundra.us.

This is a template privacy policy. Before launching publicly, have it reviewed by an attorney to ensure it accurately reflects your data practices and complies with applicable law (CCPA, GDPR, etc.).
